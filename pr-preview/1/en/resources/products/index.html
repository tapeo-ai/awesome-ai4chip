Commercial Products and Platforms

Official product and technical resources from EDA vendors and AI chip design platforms.

Commercial AI tools for digital design, verification, analog design, and physical implementation.

EDA Vendors

  • Cadence AgentStack / JedAI — Coordinates collaboration among domain agents; JedAI supplies the underlying data, models, and platform infrastructure.
  • Cadence AuraStack AI Super Agent — Extends agent workflows to PCB and advanced packaging design.
  • Cadence Cerebrus — Uses machine learning to explore digital implementation parameters and flows to optimize chip PPA.
  • Cadence ChipStack AI Super Agent — Targets digital front-end design and verification, covering specifications, RTL, testbenches, regression testing, and debugging. Mental Model and Workflows
  • Cadence InnoStack AI Super Agent — Orchestrates back-end flows, analyzes constraints, and optimizes PPA for digital implementation and signoff.
  • Cadence Maestro (2026) — Combines domain-post-trained models, decision models, and agent skills with EDA/SDA solver feedback and RL for chip and system design; initially available to selected early-access customers.
  • Cadence ViraStack AI Super Agent — Organizes design exploration, simulation analysis, and verification workflows for custom and analog ICs.
  • GPT-Synopsys (2026) — OpenAI and Synopsys are developing specialized models for EDA tool use, with planned integration into customer harnesses, Synopsys.ai, and Autopilot.
  • Siemens Aprisa AI — Integrates PPA design-space exploration and natural-language assistance into RTL-to-GDS implementation, generating tool configurations and flow examples.
  • Siemens Calibre Vision AI — Combines OASIS results with AI spatial-pattern clustering to organize large volumes of DRC violations into actionable debugging findings for teams.
  • Siemens Fuse EDA AI — Connects design tools through multimodal EDA data, RAG, MCP, and Agent Skills, organizing hierarchical planning, dynamic tool discovery, and feedback loops. Continuous Verification Architecture
  • Siemens Questa One — Combines AI-assisted verification with an Agentic Toolkit for verification planning, execution, debugging, and coverage analysis.
  • Siemens Solido AI — Applies generative and agentic AI to schematics, simulation, variation-aware verification, library characterization, and layout, with Fuse coordinating workflows across tools.
  • Synopsys AgentEngineer / Autopilot — A portfolio of agents for long-running engineering tasks across design stages, planned for availability by the end of 2026. AMD / Microsoft Case Studies
  • Synopsys.ai — Includes AI products for design-space, verification, test, and analog design optimization, connecting DSO.ai, VSO.ai, TSO.ai, and ASO.ai. Copilot
  • UniVista Design Agent (UDA) — Organizes AI workflows around design requirements, RTL, verification, and debugging, with EDA tool integration.

AI Chip Design Platforms

  • Agentrys Studio — Provides multi-agent workflow building, EDA tool connections, iterative evaluation, and team deployment.
  • ChipAgents — An agent platform for chip design and verification that also introduces the Build-A-Chip-Bench long-running workflow evaluation initiative.
  • Chipmind — Uses engineering context and EDA tools to assist SoC design, integration, and debugging.
  • ChipNexus NEX — Formerly Primis AI, a commercial chip design agent framework with command-line, collaboration, and multi-tool orchestration support.
  • Cognichip — An ACI platform for semiconductor design that integrates generation, modification, verification, and optimization into a unified workflow.
  • Moores Lab AI — Provides VerifAgent, DebugAgent, and CoverageAgent for testbench generation, debugging, and coverage improvement loops.
  • Silimate — AI agents for RTL front-end design that combine functional debugging with PPA optimization.
