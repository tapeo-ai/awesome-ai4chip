Non-LLM Models

Circuit generation, policy networks, graph representation learning, and surrogate models.

Specialized Generative Models and Policy Networks

  • AnalogGenie (2025) — Represents circuits as device-pin sequences and trains an approximately 12M-parameter autoregressive Transformer from scratch to generate analog circuit topologies. Paper Data
  • AstRL (2026) — Uses a graph neural network as a transistor-topology policy, combining expert behavior cloning, PPO, and simulation feedback to generate analog and mixed-signal circuits.
  • LEGO-Size (2025) — NVIDIA pretrains a specialized Transformer on library-cell sequences with self-supervision, follows with supervised learning, and uses GPU-based differentiable timing analysis to optimize gate sizes. Paper

Circuit Representations and Surrogate Models

  • DeepGate4 (2025) — Combines a GNN encoder with a sparse graph Transformer, using gate-level and graph-level multitask supervision to learn functional and structural representations of large AIGs. Paper
  • Multimodal Thermal Solver for 3DICs (2025) — UMD and Synopsys combine block-level thermal response learning, multimodal mapping, and linear superposition to predict thermal fields across chip sizes and power distributions. Technical Talk
  • Pieceformer (2025) — Georgia Tech and Cadence combine message passing, partitioned graph Transformers, and contrastive self-supervision to measure netlist similarity and reuse partitioning experience.
  • TRACE (2026) — Encodes RTL and netlists with a Transformer that follows logical levels, learning circuit representations through function-shift prediction and contrastive learning. Paper Data
