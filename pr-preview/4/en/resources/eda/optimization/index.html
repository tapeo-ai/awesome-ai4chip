EDA Optimization

Circuit representation and performance prediction, RTL and logic optimization, physical design, and analog circuit design.

Covers circuit representation and performance prediction, RTL and logic optimization, physical design, and analog circuit design, using representation learning, reinforcement learning, search, and differentiable optimization.

Circuit Representation & Performance Prediction

  • DeepGate4 (2025) — Combines a GNN encoder with a sparse graph Transformer, using gate-level and graph-level multitask supervision to learn functional and structural representations of large AIGs. Paper
  • Multimodal Thermal Solver for 3DICs (2025) — UMD and Synopsys combine block-level thermal response learning, multimodal mapping, and linear superposition to predict thermal fields across chip sizes and power distributions. Technical Talk
  • Pieceformer (2025) — Georgia Tech and Cadence combine message passing, partitioned graph Transformers, and contrastive self-supervision to measure netlist similarity and reuse partitioning experience.
  • TRACE (2026) — Encodes RTL and netlists with a Transformer that follows logical levels, learning circuit representations through function-shift prediction and contrastive learning. Paper Data

RTL & Logic Optimization

  • ASPEN (2025) — Cornell, NVIDIA, and UMD use LLMs to propose and select e-graph rewrite rules, then use PPA feedback to search for functionally equivalent RTL datapaths. Paper
  • SynAct (2026) — Reads live synthesis reports and tool knowledge to select commands in a feedback loop and optimize timing and PPA.

Physical Design Optimization

  • AlphaChip / Circuit Training — Uses distributed deep reinforcement learning for chip macro placement, with an open training framework and checkpoints.
  • C3PO (2026) — NVIDIA jointly optimizes timing, routability, and wirelength with GPU-based differentiable optimization, adjusting objective weights during placement iterations. Paper
  • CROP (2025) — Duke and Synopsys turn hierarchical RTL summaries into design vectors, retrieve parameter settings from similar circuits, and use RAG and LLMs to guide EDA flow tuning.
  • DREAMPlace — Uses deep learning frameworks and GPUs to accelerate global and detailed placement, providing infrastructure for learning-based physical design.
  • LEGO-Size (2025) — NVIDIA pretrains a specialized Transformer on library-cell sequences with self-supervision, follows with supervised learning, and uses GPU-based differentiable timing analysis to optimize gate sizes. Paper
  • OpenROAD AutoTuner — Tunes RTL-to-GDS flow parameters through distributed hyperparameter search and collects PPA metrics.

Analog Circuit Design

  • AnalogGenie (2025) — Represents circuits as device-pin sequences and trains an approximately 12M-parameter autoregressive Transformer from scratch to generate analog circuit topologies. Paper Data
  • AstRL (2026) — Uses a graph neural network as a transistor-topology policy, combining expert behavior cloning, PPO, and simulation feedback to generate analog and mixed-signal circuits.
