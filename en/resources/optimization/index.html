Synthesis and Physical Design Optimization

Learning, search, and optimization methods for logic synthesis, placement, and physical implementation.

This section covers reinforcement learning, search, and differentiable optimization, including infrastructure for building learning-based design methods.

  • AlphaChip / Circuit Training — Uses distributed deep reinforcement learning for chip macro placement, with an open training framework and checkpoints.
  • ASPEN (2025) — Cornell, NVIDIA, and UMD use LLMs to propose and select e-graph rewrite rules, then use PPA feedback to search for functionally equivalent RTL datapaths. Paper
  • C3PO (2026) — NVIDIA jointly optimizes timing, routability, and wirelength with GPU-based differentiable optimization, adjusting objective weights during placement iterations. Paper
  • CROP (2025) — Duke and Synopsys turn hierarchical RTL summaries into design vectors, retrieve parameter settings from similar circuits, and use RAG and LLMs to guide EDA flow tuning.
  • DREAMPlace — Uses deep learning frameworks and GPUs to accelerate global and detailed placement, providing infrastructure for learning-based physical design.
  • OpenROAD AutoTuner — Tunes RTL-to-GDS flow parameters through distributed hyperparameter search and collects PPA metrics.
  • SynAct (2026) — Reads live synthesis reports and tool knowledge to select commands in a feedback loop and optimize timing and PPA.
